Tell HireBuddy the work you want

One sentence is enough: what you can do, where you live, when you are free and what you need. HireBuddy finds jobs that suit you, applies, books interviews and checks everything twice.

Made for blind and low-vision job seekers. It works with your screen reader, with a keyboard alone, and in WorkBuddy.

Who are you?
For example: I'm blind and use NVDA, I live in Subang Jaya, I'm good on the phone, free weekdays 9 to 5.

Send to WorkBuddy through Telegram

If your WorkBuddy is connected to a Telegram bot (in WorkBuddy: Assistant, Assistant Settings, Channels, Telegram), type the bot's username. This browser remembers it. After that, Hand it to WorkBuddy opens that chat with the task typed in, on this computer or on your phone, and you press Send.

It ends in "bot", for example nadia_workbuddy_bot.
