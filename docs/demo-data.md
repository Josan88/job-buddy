# Demo data

HireBuddy ships with **17 fictional jobs** and **16 fictional job-seeker profiles** so judges can test job searching, matching, accessibility checks, employer screening, and manual application selection.

**Jobs.** The shop names are made up and the descriptions are written in our own words. The role, area, pay and shift pattern of each job are modelled on public part-time listings in the Klang Valley, which we read by opening the listing pages normally on 10 Oct 2026. We did not scrape them, and no listing text or company name was copied. MIXUE only appears when a user types it in a prompt.

**Job seekers.** All 16 profiles are fictional. Their email addresses use `example.com`, so messages to those addresses are never delivered.

Across the listings that showed an hourly rate, entry-level part-time pay in the Klang Valley ranged from RM9 to RM12 an hour, with retail ambassador roles at RM12–18. One weekend cashier listing offered RM50–60 a day for weekend shifts. For an 8-hour shift, that is below the RM65.38/day legal minimum used by HireBuddy (Minimum Wages Order 2024, P.U.(A) 376, 6-day week), so the wage check marks the job unsuitable.

## Jobs for blind and low-vision seekers (added 10 Oct 2026)

Eight jobs were created specifically to demonstrate accessibility matching: TalkLine Support, Kodlab Studio, Suara Notes, Prima Insure Agency, Klinik Mesra Sunway, Tenang Wellness, Cerita Digital, and KedaiKita Grocer. Their employers are fictional, their roles and pay are illustrative, and their station names are real stops chosen to make the walking-distance check meaningful. They are not copied from individual listings. Nadia Rahim, Nur Aqilah, and Ravi Chandran are also fictional.

Job searches never submit applications or book interviews automatically. HireBuddy checks the jobs and shows suitable choices. An application is sent only after the user selects a supported job card.
